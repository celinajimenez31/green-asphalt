Health Concerns Reported By Critics of Green Asphalt

For the past several months, residents of Greenpoint and Blissville have been in a battle against Green Asphalt, a recycled asphalt plant they say has been emitting fumes that have caused a variety of health issues.

By Celina Jimenez

The plant has received orders from the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) to raise the height of their stacks to 90 feet by December 11, as well as test their emissions for 25 contaminants immediately after said raise is complete. The 25 contaminants were outlined in a June 2025 letter sent from the DEC to Green Asphalt.

Though it has not yet been confirmed whether Green Asphalt's emissions contain any of the aforementioned contaminants, many of the symptoms reported by residents are known side effects of some or many of those contaminants. Below are three of the most frequent complaints reported by residents, all of whom believe these issues to be a direct result of Green Asphalt's emissions.
